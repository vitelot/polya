Polya's Urn with Innovations

Visualisation of the innovation model from Tria et al., Scientific Reports 4, 5890 (2014). An urn starts with one red ball. Each step: a ball is drawn at random; ρ copies are returned (reinforcement); if the color is novel, ν+1 brand-new balls expand the adjacent possible. The pulsing ball is the one drawn; new colors enter from the right, copies from the left.
